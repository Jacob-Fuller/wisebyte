Privacy policy

Wisebyte Kids · Last updated 28 September 2026

Wisebyte Kids is a learning app for children aged 5 to 10. We built it so that it collects nothing about your child.

What the app stores

  • The first name or nickname you type for each player
  • The age group you choose
  • Stars, stickers, lessons completed, best scores and streaks

This is saved only on this device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or email addresses
  • No advertising
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party code
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access
  • No chat or sharing with other users
  • No links out of the app, except to the App Store or Microsoft Store to manage a subscription, which sits behind the grown-ups gate

Subscription

Wisebyte Kids is available by monthly subscription ($4.99), or as part of Wisebyte All Access, which also unlocks our other Wisebyte apps for grown-ups. Subscriptions can only be started, restored or managed from behind the grown-ups gate. They are sold and managed by Apple or Microsoft, and we never receive payment details. If All Access is active, the app keeps a small note on the device so the other Wisebyte apps can unlock too. It contains no personal information.

Read to me

The "Read to me" button uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. The words are read out by your device. We don't record or keep them.

Deleting data

Use "Remove player" in the grown-ups area, or delete the app. Either way the data is gone for good, because there is no other copy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@wisebyte.app

This policy follows the Australian Privacy Principles and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
