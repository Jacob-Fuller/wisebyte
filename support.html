Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Wisebyte and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I already subscribed. How do I get access back on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the subscription screen in Wisebyte and tap Restore Purchases.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app clears your progress and streak.

Is the content advice?
No. Lessons are general information for learning and interest, not financial, legal or medical advice.
