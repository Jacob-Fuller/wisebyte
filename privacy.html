Privacy Policy

Wisebyte · Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version: Wisebyte doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Wisebyte is published by Jacob Fuller ("we", "us"), based in Queensland, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You can contact us at contact@wisebyte.app.

Information we don't collect

Wisebyte doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. We don't use analytics, advertising networks, tracking tools or third-party SDKs that collect data about you. We never see your name, email, location, contacts or usage.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, Wisebyte saves your learning progress on your device: things like lessons completed, quiz scores, your streak, XP, saved words, achievements and settings. This information:

  • is stored only on your device, using the app's private storage;
  • may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy;
  • is deleted if you use "Reset all progress" in the app, or delete the app.

Subscriptions and payments

Wisebyte is available by monthly subscription. Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store), depending on where you got the app. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether your account has an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how they handle your information.

Children

Wisebyte is designed for adults and older teens. It isn't directed at children under 13 (our separate Wisebyte Kids app is made for them), and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contacting us

If you email us, we'll use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we won't share them. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Wisebyte handles information, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change means we would start collecting personal information, we'll tell you in the app before it happens.

Questions or complaints

Email contact@wisebyte.app and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
