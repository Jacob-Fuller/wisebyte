Privacy Policy

Wisebyte · Last updated 3 October 2026

The short version: Wisebyte doesn't sell or share your personal information, and has no advertising or tracking. An account is optional: if you create one, we keep your email address so you can sign in. Your learning progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Wisebyte ("we", "us") is based in Queensland, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You can contact us at contact@wisebyte.app.

Your account (optional)

You can use Wisebyte without an account. If you create one, we collect your email address and a password so you can sign in. Your password is never stored in readable form: it is protected with one-way hashing by our sign-in provider, Supabase, which stores account details for us on secure servers. We use your email only to run your account: to confirm it's yours, to send sign-in and password-reset codes, and to reply if you contact us. We don't send marketing emails.

When you're signed in, the app keeps a sign-in token on your device so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Deleting your account: open the Account tab in the app and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and email address from our systems. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Information we don't collect

We don't use analytics, advertising networks, tracking tools or third-party SDKs that collect data about you. We never see your location, contacts, or what you learn in the app.

What stays on your device

To make the app work, Wisebyte saves your learning progress on your device: things like lessons completed, quiz scores, your streak, XP, saved words, achievements and settings. This information:

  • is stored only on your device, using the app's private storage;
  • may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy;
  • is deleted if you use "Reset all progress" in the app, or delete the app.

The Wisebyte Scroll feed also saves the cards you keep, which cards you've read and your reminder setting on your device, in the same way. If you turn on its daily reminder, your phone schedules it; no server is involved.

Subscriptions and payments

Wisebyte is available by monthly subscription through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your account has an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your information.

Children

Wisebyte is designed for adults and older teens. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contacting us

If you email us, we'll use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we won't share them. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Wisebyte handles information, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change means we would start collecting personal information, we'll tell you in the app before it happens.

Questions or complaints

Email contact@wisebyte.app and we'll respond as soon as possible. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
