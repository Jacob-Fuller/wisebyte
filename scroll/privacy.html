Privacy Policy

Wisebyte - Scroll · Last updated 27 September 2026

Wisebyte - Scroll is designed so that nothing about you leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

The cards you keep, which cards you’ve read, your reading counts and your reminder setting are saved only on your phone so the app can remember them. Deleting the app removes them. You can also clear them any time in Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Wisebyte - Scroll is available by monthly subscription, either on its own or as part of Wisebyte All Access, which also unlocks our other Wisebyte apps. Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store), depending on where you got the app. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether your account has an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how they handle your information.

If you have Wisebyte All Access, the app saves a small note on your device (on iPhone and iPad, in storage shared only between our own Wisebyte apps) saying that All Access is active and when that was last confirmed, so your other Wisebyte apps on the same device can unlock too. It contains no personal information.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved.

Sharing

When you share a card, the text goes only to the app you choose, using your phone’s standard share sheet.

Children

Wisebyte - Scroll is designed for adults and older teens. It isn't directed at children under 13 (our separate Wisebyte Kids app is made for them), and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and on our website.
