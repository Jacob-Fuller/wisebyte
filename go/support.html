Common questions

How do I listen without signal?
Tap the download arrow next to any lesson, or use "Download trip" on the Listen screen, "Download" on a course, or "Download all" under Me. Downloaded lessons show an Offline badge and play anywhere.

Downloads use a lot of space. How do I clear them?
Go to Me, then Downloads, then Remove all. You can also remove single lessons with the tick next to them.

Can I change the narrator?
Yes. Pick Female (Heart) or Male (Michael) on the Listen screen, the Me screen, or inside the player. You'll keep your place in the lesson.

Does it keep playing when my screen is locked?
Yes. Use your lock screen, headphones or car controls to pause, skip back 15 seconds, or jump between parts.

What's Drive mode?
Tap the car icon at the top of the player. It's a dark screen with three big buttons, for quick taps only when it's safe and legal. Set up your lesson before you drive, and follow your state's road rules for phone use.

Is the content advice?
No. Lessons are general information for learning and interest, not financial, legal or medical advice.

How do I reset my progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears your history, streak and downloads.
