Privacy Policy

Wisebyte - Go · Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version: Wisebyte - Go has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your listening history stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it works the way you left it: your chosen narrator and playback speed, where you're up to in each lesson, which lessons you've finished, your daily listening minutes and streak, and any lessons you download. This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information. We don't use advertising or analytics services, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

Streaming and downloads

When you play or download a lesson, your device requests the audio file from our file server. Like any website, that server may briefly record technical details such as your IP address and the time of the request, for security and to keep the service running. These records aren't linked to you and aren't used to build a profile.

Subscriptions and payments

Wisebyte - Go is available by monthly subscription, either on its own or as part of Wisebyte All Access, which also unlocks our other Wisebyte apps. Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store), depending on where you got the app. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether your account has an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how they handle your information.

If you have Wisebyte All Access, the app saves a small note on your device (on iPhone and iPad, in storage shared only between our own Wisebyte apps) saying that All Access is active and when that was last confirmed, so your other Wisebyte apps on the same device can unlock too. It contains no personal information.

Children

Wisebyte - Go is designed for adults and older teens. It isn't directed at children under 13 (our separate Wisebyte Kids app is made for them), and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We handle information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Because we don't hold personal information about you, there's usually nothing for us to access or correct, but you're welcome to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
